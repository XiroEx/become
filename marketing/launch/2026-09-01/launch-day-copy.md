## The sceptical FAQ

Written to be quoted out of context. Each answer is complete in one paragraph and honest about the
limit, because honesty about limits is the strongest credibility move available to a product with
no user counts to show.

**1. Is it really free?**
Yes. Free today, no card, no trial clock, no paid tier. There is no price and none is being
implied for later; if that ever changes it will be said plainly, not slipped in.

**2. No password? Is that safe?**
You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email, and it expires in fifteen minutes. There is
no password to reuse, leak, or forget. Google sign-in and passkeys work too. Your email is the
account, which is why we guard the sending domain carefully.

**3. Does it count my reps for me?**
No. You type the set in. What LIVE mode does is put last session's weight and reps under the field
you are typing into, flag a PR when it is your best on that lift, and start the rest timer when
you tick the set off. Logging is one tap, not a memory test. Nothing watches you train.

**4. Where does my food photo go?**
The photo is sent to Become's server, read into a list of items with their calories and macros,
and saved with your own log so you can correct any line later. It is your data in your account:
it is never shown to other members and never used in marketing. *(George verifies the retention
detail against the plate-scan pipeline before signing - do not add a "deleted immediately" claim
unless the code actually deletes it.)*

**5. Is it in the App Store?**
No, and there is nothing to download. Become runs in the browser and installs to your home screen,
where it opens full screen like an app and can send notifications. That is also why there is no
star rating to show you.

**6. Do I need equipment?**
No specific kit. The coach-built programs say what they use, and when the plan does not fit the
room you are standing in, the generator builds a session from the focus, your level, and only the
equipment actually in front of you.

**The photo-logging limit, stated once for anyone who asks how accurate it is:** it estimates
portions, so it reads a chicken breast better than a mixed curry, and you correct anything it
missed in a couple of taps. We have not measured an accuracy percentage and will not quote one.

## Guardrails carried from the other files

- Any injury, medical, or pregnancy question gets the referral answer and nothing clever after it.
- Never a number about users, results, pounds, or timelines. "It is early, that is why I am
  telling you first" is the true answer to "how many people use it".
- Never "free for now". Never a program name that Jon has not confirmed is live (O-5).
- A "did not work for me" report: ask for a screenshot, send it to George, log it in
  `measurement.md` (it is guardrail 1).
