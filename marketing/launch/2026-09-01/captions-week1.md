## How these were written

**First line does the work.** Instagram hides the caption behind a "more" tap at roughly 125
characters. The whole caption is searchable, the first line is what gets read, so the search phrase
is in it. Phrases targeted, all of which the product genuinely satisfies: *app that logs food from a
photo*, *workout app that remembers your last weight*, *beginner gym program*, *AI workout
generator*, *how to log a superset*.

**One CTA per post, on the ladder.** Send, then comment keyword, then save, then profile link. "Link
in bio" is never the primary CTA on a Reel. Keyword posts ship only on days somebody is around to
hand-reply; we run no DM-automation tool.

**Hashtags are categorisation, not distribution.** Five to eight on Instagram, three to four on
TikTok, all specific, all at the end, no wall of thirty, no reach-bait tags. The search phrase lives
in line one where it is read, not in a hashtag where it is not.

**Alt text is one line, describes what is in the frame, and never repeats the caption.**
