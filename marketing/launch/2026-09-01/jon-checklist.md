# Jon - what you actually do, day by day

Launch day is **Tuesday, September 1**. Today is Tuesday, August 25.

You are the launch. The app has been live for months and nobody has been told, and the people most
likely to try it are the people who already know you. Everything else on this list is scaffolding
around you posting three times on September 1 and answering people.

Two things I need you to hold onto:

1. **Never say a number about the app.** Not how many people use it, not how much weight anyone
   lost, not how long anything takes. If someone asks how many people use it, "it is early, that is
   why I am telling you first" is the true answer and it is a better one.
2. **It is free. Full stop.** Not free for now, not free trial, not free tier. If someone asks
   whether it will cost money later, say you do not know yet, because you do not.

Full plan is in `launch-plan.md`. Shot specs, word for word, are in `reels-pack.md`. Captions are
in `captions-week1.md`. You do not need to read any of those to do this list.

### The shot rules that matter

- **Film vertical.** Phone upright.
- **Keep your head and the text in the middle of the frame.** The top and bottom of the screen get
  covered by Instagram's own buttons.
- Full brightness on the screen if a phone screen is in shot.
- Nothing filmed from your camera roll. Everything we use, we shoot on Saturday, on purpose.
- If a take goes wrong, say "again" out loud and restart. Do not stop recording.

### If Saturday falls apart

If you get one thing on camera, make it **LAUNCH-HERO**, one take, Sunday morning, filmed on your
own phone with nobody helping. We can run the launch on that alone. We cannot run it on nothing.

## The things that would genuinely hurt us

Short list. Everything else is recoverable.

- **A number about results.** Pounds lost, weeks to a result, how many people use it. Not in a
  video, not in a caption, not in a DM.
- **A made-up client story.** Even a small one, even a composite.
- **Medical advice.** Refer out, every time.
- **Saying it is free with an asterisk.** "Free for now" invites the question we cannot answer.
- **Promising a reply from you inside the app.** The chat feature is not switched on. If someone
  wants to talk to you, it is your DMs.
- **Going quiet on launch day.** A post with no replies underneath it reads worse than no post.
