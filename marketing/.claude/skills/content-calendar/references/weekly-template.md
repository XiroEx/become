# Weekly Templates

Three shapes. Pick by production capacity, not by ambition. A template that collapses in week
three costs more than a smaller one that runs for a year.

---

## Template A — Five slots (default, roughly 6-8 hours a week)

| Day | Slot | Pillar | Format | Owner | CTA | Typical asset source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Watch It Work | Mechanism | Reel 15-30s | Brand | Send | Film session |
| Wed | One Tap | Teaching | Carousel 4-6 | Brand | Save | `webapp/public/screenshots/v2/` + annotation |
| Thu | Coach Answer | Coach | Reel 30-45s | Jon | Send | Film session |
| Fri | Plan The Week | Planning | Carousel or Reel | Brand | `WEEK1` | Captures + `remotion-assets` |
| Sun | Read Your Week | Recap | Reel or carousel | Brand | Save | `progress-*.webp` |

Monthly totals: roughly 8 Reels, 4-5 carousels. Statics only in stories.

## Template B — Three slots (roughly 3 hours a week)

| Day | Slot | Pillar | Format | Owner | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue | Watch It Work | Mechanism | Reel 15-30s | Brand | Send |
| Thu | Coach Answer | Coach | Reel 30-45s | Jon | Send |
| Sun | One Tap | Teaching | Carousel 4-6 | Brand | Save |

Run this when capacity is genuinely three hours. Three slots held for six months beat five slots
held for five weeks. Recap content folds into the Sunday carousel once a month rather than weekly.

## Template C — Launch week

The launch's full run of show belongs to `launch-campaign`. This is only the social layer.

| Day | Slot | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-3 | Watch It Work | Reel | Mechanism demo of the shipping feature, no announcement language |
| T-1 | Story sequence | Stories | Three frames, App Tip badge, teaching the tap |
| T-0 | One Tap | Carousel | The full how-to, CTA on the last slide |
| T-0 | Watch It Work | Reel | The payoff cut of the same mechanism, different hook |
| T+2 | Coach Answer | Reel | Jon answers the question the launch actually raised |
| T+5 | Plan The Week | Carousel | Where the feature sits inside a normal week |

Rules for launch week: the feature must be live on `become.redbtn.io` before T-3, captures are
retaken after the final UI lands, and no more than two posts per day on any platform.

## Slot mechanics

**Draft-by dates.** If publishing is manual, add a draft-by date two days before the post date.
If publishing is scheduled, add a scheduled-by date three days before.

**The swing slot.** Leave one slot per week unassigned at planning time. It absorbs whatever the
month actually produces: a good question in the comments, a clip that came out better than
expected, a product change. A calendar with no swing slot forces bad rows into good dates.

**Missed slots.** A missed named slot stays visible as a missed row. Do not backfill with a
double post; the reach split makes both weaker. Miss two in a row and cut the template one slot.

**Cross-posting.** One row per platform. The same asset on Instagram and TikTok is two rows, and
the TikTok cut usually wants a different first 1.5 seconds and a different caption keyword.

---

## Story layer

Stories are not on the calendar as individual rows. They are a standing rule:

- Every feed post gets a story reshare within four hours, with a different framing question.
- One App Tip story sequence per week, three to five frames, on the same topic as the Wednesday
  carousel. This is the highest-value story pattern in `marketing/inspo-analysis.md`.
- Polls and question stickers are for gathering the next Coach Answer, not for engagement bait.
- Never a story that shows a real user's data, and never a repost of member content without
  written permission (see `ugc-creator-briefs`).

## Capacity arithmetic

Estimate before committing. Rough per-post costs at competence, excluding batch savings:

| Item | Time |
|---|---|
| Mechanism demo Reel: film, cut, caption | 60-90 min |
| Coach Answer Reel: film (in batch), cut, caption | 30-45 min |
| Teaching carousel from existing captures | 45-60 min |
| Recap post from existing captures | 30 min |
| Story reshare | 5 min |

Batching a film session cuts per-post filming time by roughly half after the first two. If the
arithmetic exceeds stated capacity, cut slots. Do not plan on heroics.
