# Share Artifact Candidates, Scored

## The four tests

Every artifact must pass all four. Three out of four does not ship.

1. **Legible out of context** — a stranger who knows nothing understands it in two seconds.
2. **Flattering to the sharer** — nobody sends something that makes them look worse.
3. **Honest about the data** — every number was generated by the sharer. No embellishment.
4. **Branded, not an ad** — the mark is present and small. If it reads as an ad, it is not sent.

## Scorecard

| Artifact | Legible | Flattering | Honest | Subtle brand | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly recap card | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **Ship.** Best recurring artifact |
| Single PR card | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **Ship.** Best spontaneous artifact |
| Streak milestone card | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **Ship.** Milestones already exist at 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 200, 365 |
| Plate breakdown | ✅ | ⚠️ | ✅ | ✅ | **Conditional.** Genuinely surprising mechanic, but food is personal. Opt-in only |
| Session snapshot (`/share/<shareId>`) | ⚠️ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **Ship with a summary card.** Already built; needs a legible preview |
| Program snapshot | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **Ship.** Already built, and the most useful thing to send a friend |
| Mind session (`/share/mind/<token>`) | ⚠️ | ⚠️ | ✅ | ✅ | **Conditional.** Mind data is the most private category. Opt-in, never prompted |
| Weight-change card | ✅ | ❌ | ⚠️ | ✅ | **Refused.** See below |
| Full workout log table | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **No.** A document, not an artifact |
| Leaderboard or comparison | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | **Refused.** Exposes other members |
| Before/after | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | **Refused.** Implies a guaranteed outcome |

**Why the weight-change card is refused.** It fails "flattering" for most people most of the
time, it invites a results claim we cannot make, and it drags the brand toward before/after
framing that the constraint list prohibits. A member may share their own progress chart from
inside the app if they choose to screenshot it. We do not build a one-tap artifact that
encourages it.

## Artifact specs

### Weekly recap card

- **Format:** 1080x1920 story, plus a 1080x1080 square. Light and dark variants ship together.
- **Content:** one hero number, one supporting line, the date range, a small mark.
- **Empty handling:** suppress any block with no data. Never render `0 sessions` or `0.0 lb`.
  Trend language needs at least three data points in the window.
- **The Becoming:** may be referenced once, small. Not the theme of the card.

```
✅  3 SESSIONS
    Week of Aug 18. Streak: 12 days.
    become

❌  YOUR TRANSFORMATION JOURNEY CONTINUES!
    Sessions: 3 | Weight: 0.0 lb | Mood: 0 | Water: 0
    Become — the app that changes everything
```

### Single PR card

- **Format:** 1080x1920 story primary.
- **Content:** exercise name, weight x reps, the date, one line of context if a previous best
  exists.
- **Honesty:** only a genuine PR against the member's own logged history. Never against a
  population average.

```
✅  BENCH PRESS
    185 lb x 5
    Previous best: 175 x 5
    become
```

### Streak milestone card

- **Format:** square and story.
- **Content:** the number, the unit, nothing else.
- **Colour:** gold, per the brand system. Streaks and The Becoming own gold.
- **Timing:** fires on the milestones the product already recognises (3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100,
  200, 365 in `webapp/lib/email.ts`). Do not invent a parallel milestone ladder.

### Session and program snapshot

Already built. `Share` stores a frozen, hydrated payload so the public page needs no auth and no
coupling to the sharer's live data. `sanitizeExercise` and `sanitizeWorkout` in
`webapp/lib/share.ts` normalise the payload, including group types (superset, circuit, triset,
giant set, EMOM, AMRAP).

**What it needs:** a legible summary at the top of the page and as the link preview. A stranger
should see `Push A. 5 exercises, about 40 minutes. Shared by Alex.` before they see fourteen
rows.

**Open Graph.** The share page already sets a title and description from the snapshot. Give it a
matching OG image so a pasted link in a DM or a group chat renders as a card rather than a bare
URL. That is the single cheapest improvement to stage 3 of the loop.

## Design system for share artifacts

Use the real brand tokens. Do not invent a share-specific palette.

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Training green | `#16a34a` / `#22c55e`. Sessions, PRs, programs |
| Violet | AI and Mind surfaces |
| Gold | Streaks and The Becoming |
| Type | Geist. Maximum two weights per frame |
| Themes | Light **and** dark variants ship together. Never dark-only |
| Mark | Small, bottom, consistent position. Never a logo lockup at 30% of the frame |
| Safe area | Story crops keep content clear of the top and bottom 250px, where platform UI sits |

Composite patterns, export sizes, and quality targets live in `image-production`. Multi-layer or
animated versions go through `remotion-assets`. **Check `marketing/out/` and
`webapp/public/screenshots/v2/` before rendering anything new.**

## Share copy that ships with the artifact

The share sheet should preload text the member can edit or send as-is. Write it in their voice,
not ours, and keep it short enough to survive a DM.

| Artifact | Preloaded text | Alternate | Rationale for the alternate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recap | `3 sessions this week.` | `Week done.` | Shorter suits a story caption; the number suits a DM |
| PR | `185 x 5. First time.` | `Finally got 185.` | The second reads more human and less like a log entry |
| Streak | `12 days.` | `Two weeks tomorrow.` | Forward-looking framing invites a reply |
| Program | `This is the program I'm running.` | `Want to run this with me?` | The second one converts better and asks for a partner |

**Never** preload a promotional line. `Check out Become, the all-in-one fitness app!` is not
something a person sends to a friend, and it makes the sharer look like an ad.

```
❌ I'm crushing it with Become! Download now and start your journey! 💪🔥
✅ 185 x 5. First time.
```

## Privacy rules, non-negotiable

- The share payload is a frozen snapshot of the **sharer's own** data. Never anyone else's.
- No member's name, numbers, or streak appears in another member's artifact.
- Mind and nutrition data are the most sensitive categories. Opt-in only, never prompted at a
  moment of vulnerability.
- A share link is public and unauthenticated by design. Say so plainly in the share UI so the
  member knows what they are creating.
- Beta and production share one database. A share created on beta is a real, live, public link.
- Never render a token, an ID that leaks other records, or any credential into an artifact.
