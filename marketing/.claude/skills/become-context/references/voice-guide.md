# Become Voice Guide

The rules every skill enforces on its own output. Copy the summary into
`marketing/.agents/become-context.md` section 10 and keep this file as the long form.

## The one line

**Confident, concrete, zero fluff, empowering not preachy. "Evidence, not vibes."**

The user is not lazy. Their tools were scattered. Every line should read as if written by
someone who respects them and has already done the work.

## Brand words

**simple · sleek · innovative · empowering**

These are constraints, not adjectives to print. "Simple" means the sentence is short. "Sleek"
means nothing decorative survives the edit. "Innovative" means we point at the mechanic, not the
word. "Empowering" means the reader ends the line more capable, not more guilty.

## Two registers, never mixed in one block

| | Product voice | Coach voice (Jon) |
|---|---|---|
| Person | Second person, present tense | First person |
| Leads with | The mechanic | The experience |
| Example | "Point the camera at your set. Become counts the reps." | "I stopped counting reps out loud years ago. Half my clients lose the count on set three." |
| Owned by | `copywriting` | `coach-brand-voice` |

A landing section, a push, a button, an email body: product voice. A talking-head script, a
caption fronted by Jon, a reply to a member: coach voice.

## Sentence rules

- Second person, present tense, active voice.
- Short sentences. Vary the length so it does not read like a list.
- Lead with the concrete noun.
- Verbs the product actually does: log, scan, plan, count, recap, generate, show, queue, track.
- **Near-zero em dashes.** Use a period, a comma, or a colon.
- No emoji in product-voice copy. A social caption may use at most one, and only when it carries
  meaning.
- Numbers only when they are the product's own numbers (12-week programs, 42 demo clips) and
  never as an outcome claim.

## Banned list, with the replacement

A prohibition without a replacement gets ignored. Each row gives the move that works instead.

| Banned | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| journey | Says nothing, and every fitness app says it | The specific week, session, or lift |
| unlock your potential | Empty and slightly condescending | The concrete capability: "See what you lifted last Tuesday." |
| game-changer | Reviewer language, not product language | Name the mechanic |
| revolutionary | Unprovable | Name the mechanic |
| seamless | Vendor filler | Say what does not happen: "No second app." |
| effortless | Implies the work is not work | "One photo." "One tap." |
| 10x | Fake precision | Nothing. Cut the line. |
| crush it, beast mode, no excuses | Shame framing | Coach-neutral: "Get today's session done." |
| just, simply | Minimizes their difficulty | Delete the word. The sentence is fine. |
| transform | Promises an outcome | Describe the mechanic, let them infer |
| we're excited to announce | Throat-clearing | Lead with the thing |
| in a world where | AI slop opener | Delete the clause |
| not just X, but Y | AI slop construction | Pick one |

## Weak versus strong

```
Weak:   Start your fitness journey.
Strong: Get this week's workout.

Weak:   Transform your nutrition with effortless logging.
Strong: Photograph the plate. Become itemizes it.

Weak:   Unlock a seamless, all-in-one wellness experience.
Strong: One app instead of five.

Weak:   Our revolutionary AI creates personalized workouts.
Strong: Pick a focus, your level, and the equipment in front of you. Get a session in seconds.

Weak:   Never miss a workout again with our smart reminders!
Strong: Tomorrow: Day 2, Lower A.

Weak:   Simply tap to log your set.
Strong: Log the set. Your last numbers are already on screen.
```

❌ "Become is the revolutionary all-in-one platform that transforms your fitness journey."
✅ "Your program, your meals, your mind, your numbers. One app."

❌ "Effortlessly crush your macros with our seamless barcode scanner."
✅ "Scan the barcode. Macros land in today's total."

❌ "Don't break the streak! You've come too far to quit now."
✅ "Two sessions left this week. Day 3 is ready when you are."

❌ "Join thousands of users who have already transformed their bodies."
✅ "Built by coach Jon Don, from the programs he runs with his own clients."
(The second is a claim we can actually defend. The first is a fabricated count and a results claim.)

## Never

- Never shame. No confirmshaming buttons ("No thanks, I like being out of shape"), no guilt
  streak copy, no before/after framing that implies a guaranteed outcome.
- Never promise a timeline or a pound count.
- Never make a medical claim.
- Never invent a testimonial, a user count, a rating, or a price.
- Never write "(beta)" into public-facing copy or let it appear in a capture.
- Never let The Becoming become the headline theme. One section, one mention, at most.

## AI-slop tells to strip on sight

Tricolon addiction (three-item lists in every paragraph), "not just X but Y," symmetrical clause
pairs, stacked adjectives, em dash overuse, opening with a rhetorical question, closing with a
summary of what you already said, and any sentence whose first four words could belong to any
company on earth.
