# Hook Library

Ten hook shapes crossed with Become's hubs, grouped into four families (Training, Nutrition,
Mind, Progress and Dashboard). Every hook below is constraint-safe: no results
claim, no count, no pricing, no shaming, no before/after.

**Ranked by reported 3-second hold in fitness and lifestyle short-form** (**Tier C**: agency
teardowns and creator round-ups, no published sample, internal and directional only): specific
outcome > POV realism > unpopular opinion > direct question > generic product reveal. Treat the
ordering as a starting bet, not a finding, and let our own trial-Reel results overwrite it. Negative framing tends to out-hook positive framing on TikTok, which is exactly why the
no-shaming line matters: negative about the *situation*, never about the *person*.

---

## The ten shapes

| # | Shape | Skeleton | Why it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Specific outcome | "[Concrete result] without [the thing they hate]" | Names a payoff in second one |
| 2 | POV realism | "POV: [exact situation the viewer is in]" | Recognition beats persuasion |
| 3 | Unpopular opinion | "[Common rule] is wrong and here is the version that works" | Curiosity gap plus stakes |
| 4 | Direct question | "[Question the viewer has already asked themselves]" | Forces an internal answer |
| 5 | Mid-action open | Start mid-rep, mid-shutter, mid-scroll | Motion is the hook |
| 6 | Number on screen | "[Product-generated number] in [time]" | Concrete, verifiable, non-outcome |
| 7 | Objection first | "[Their objection], and here is what actually happens" | Disarms before it pitches |
| 8 | Result then rewind | Show the end state, then "here is how that got there" | Promises the method |
| 9 | Two-option contrast | "[Slow way] or [this]" | Comparison is instantly parseable |
| 10 | Named recipient | "If you [specific behaviour], this is for you" | Self-selection plus a send cue |

Shape 10 doubles as a send CTA. Shape 6 must only ever use a number the app produced, never an
outcome number.

## The grid: 40 written hooks

### Training (set logging in LIVE mode, PR history, programs, generator)

| Shape | Hook |
|---|---|
| Specific outcome | "Last Tuesday's weight was on the screen before I loaded the bar." |
| POV realism | "POV: you are three sets in and you cannot remember what you lifted last week." |
| Unpopular opinion | "You do not need a tracker on your wrist. You need the last number in front of you." |
| Direct question | "What did you lift on this exact machine last Tuesday?" |
| Mid-action open | Thumb typing 160 into an empty field, "Last: 155 x 10" underneath it. |
| Number on screen | "Set 3 of 3. PR 160. Logged before the plates came off." |
| Objection first | "Logging mid-session breaks your focus. Two numbers and a checkbox, watch." |
| Result then rewind | Session complete screen, then "thirty five minutes ago I had two dumbbells." |
| Two-option contrast | "Guess what you lifted last week, or read it off the screen." |
| Named recipient | "If you log sets in your Notes app, this is for you." |

## Rewrite pairs

❌ "Become is the all-in-one fitness app you have been waiting for."
✅ "Training, food and mood on one screen."

❌ "Stop making excuses and start today."
✅ "The plan did not fail. Your week changed and the plan did not."

❌ "Transform your body in 12 weeks."
✅ "Twelve weeks of sessions, in order, with a demo clip on the big lifts."

❌ "This app changed my life."
✅ "This app had my last three sets on screen before I picked the bar up."

❌ "You will not believe what this app does."
✅ "One photo, four items, macros against my targets."

❌ "Are you tired of being out of shape?"
✅ "Tired of retyping the same four foods every night?"

## Banned hook moves

- Shame, guilt, or body judgement in any form.
- Before and after, physique reveals, weight-loss counters.
- Any promised timeline, pound count, or health outcome.
- Fabricated testimonials, invented user counts, invented pricing or discounts.
- Fake urgency ("last chance," "closing soon") when nothing is closing.
- Engagement bait ("comment 1 or 2," "tag three friends to win").
- Bait-and-switch: a hook the video does not pay off.
- Naming a competitor negatively.

## Trial-Reel usage

Trial Reels show to non-followers first and under-read published performance because they lack
the follower boost. Use them for **relative hook comparison only**: same body, three different
first 1.5 seconds, 4-7 trials a week. Log the winning shape number here so the pattern compounds
instead of being rediscovered every month.
