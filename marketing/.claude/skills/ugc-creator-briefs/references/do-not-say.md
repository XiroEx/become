# Do-Not-Say List

Creator claims are our claims. Every line here is a hard prohibition with the reason and a safe
replacement. Put the relevant lines in the brief in bold; do not link to this file and hope.

---

## 1. Results and outcomes

| Do not say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "I lost 12 pounds using this" | A results claim we would have to substantiate | "I logged every session for three weeks" |
| "You'll see results in 30 days" | Promised timeline | "The program runs in phases, and each phase has a plan" |
| "This got me my best physique" | Implied guaranteed outcome | "This kept my training in one place" |
| "Guaranteed gains" | Guarantee | "Every set is logged with what you did last time" |
| "It transformed my body" | Outcome claim plus body framing | "It counted the set while my phone was on the floor" |

**Rule:** describe behaviour and mechanism, never outcome. What the app did, what you did, not
what your body became.

## 2. Health and medical

| Do not say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "It fixed my back pain" | Medical claim | Nothing. Do not mention conditions |
| "Great for injury recovery" | Medical claim | "There is a demo video with the cue on every exercise" |
| "It helped my anxiety" | Medical claim | "There is a short guided session and a mood check-in" |
| "Boosts your metabolism" | Physiological claim | "You set your own calorie and macro targets" |
| "Safe if you're pregnant" | Medical advice | Nothing. Refer to a clinician |

**Rule:** no condition, no diagnosis, no treatment, no physiological mechanism. Mind features are
described as short guided sessions and mood tracking, never as therapy or treatment.

## 3. Bodies

| Do not say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| Before and after footage | Implies a guaranteed outcome | Show the mechanism working |
| "Get rid of your belly" | Body shaming | Nothing |
| "Summer body" framing | Body shaming | "Three sessions a week that fit your week" |
| "I was so out of shape" | Self-shaming still models shaming | "I had not trained consistently in a year" |
| Weight on screen as a goal number | Outcome claim | Weight tracking exists; do not present a target as a promise |

**Rule:** no before/after, no body commentary about yourself or anyone else, no aesthetic goal as
a promise.

## 4. Price and value

| Do not say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "It's cheaper than [app]" | Implies a price. There is none | "It is free today" |
| "Worth every penny" | Implies payment | "Signup is an email link, no card" |
| "Get it before the price goes up" | Invented pricing and fake urgency | Nothing |
| "Free trial" | There is no trial; it is free today | "Free today" |
| "Premium version" | No tiers exist | Nothing |

**Rule:** the only permitted statement about price is "free today," alongside "email magic link"
and "no credit card."

## 5. Competitors

| Do not say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "[Competitor] is a scam" | Disparagement and legal exposure | Nothing |
| "Better than [competitor] at everything" | Unverifiable comparison | "It holds training and nutrition in the same place" |
| "[Competitor] steals your data" | Defamatory | Nothing |

**Rule:** describe what Become does. Never rank a named competitor negatively. Category
comparisons ("instead of four separate apps") are fine because they name no one.

## 6. Product truth

| Do not say | Why |
|---|---|
| Any feature not in the brief | It may not exist. Product truth is fixed |
| "It syncs with [wearable]" | Not in product truth |
| "It has a coach in your pocket 24/7" | Overstates what the app does |
| "AI knows exactly what you need" | Overstates the generator |
| "(beta)" or anything implying unfinished | Never in a marketing deliverable |

**Rule:** if the creator is unsure whether the app does something, they ask before filming. "Not
available today" is the honest answer and it costs nothing.

## 7. Tone

| Do not say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "No excuses" | Shaming | "The plan holds where you left off" |
| "Stop being lazy" | Shaming | "Your week changed. The plan can too" |
| "Beast mode," "crush it," "grind" | Hustle framing, off-brand | Plain description of the session |
| "Unlock your potential," "game-changer" | Banned filler | Name the mechanism |
| "Effortless," "seamless" | Banned filler | "One photo. Every item on the plate" |

**Rule:** the viewer is not lazy. Their tools were scattered. Empowering, never preachy.

## 8. Other people's data

- Never film another user's account, profile, message, or logged data.
- Never film a member's face or body without their written permission.
- Never film a minor.
- Never screen-record a group chat, a coaching thread, or a support conversation.

---

## Applying the list

- Copy the sections relevant to the creator's niche into section 7 of the brief, in bold.
- Anything on this list means a **recut**, not a caption edit. A caption cannot fix a spoken claim.
- If a claim cannot be removed by a cut, the asset does not ship, regardless of what it cost.
- The same list governs member reposts. A member's honest results claim is still a claim once we
  amplify it.
