### 7. Do-not-say

These are hard limits. Anything on this list means a recut, not a caption fix.

- No weight-loss numbers, no pound or kilo counts, no timelines ("in 30 days").
- No medical claims of any kind, including pain, injury recovery, sleep, or hormones.
- No before and after, no physique reveals, no body commentary about yourself or anyone else.
- No pricing, tiers, discounts, trials, or comparisons of cost. Become is free today.
- No "results guaranteed," "this will change your life," or any promised outcome.
- No naming a competitor negatively.
- No shaming language: "no excuses," "stop being lazy," "beast mode," "crush it."
- No footage showing another person's account data.
- Do not describe a feature that is not in this brief. If you are unsure whether the app does
  something, ask before filming.

### 8. Disclosure

Our relationship: [paid / gifted access / affiliate].

You must disclose it clearly and conspicuously:

- **Spoken:** once, plainly, in the first ten seconds. Example wording: "Become is paying me to show you this."
- **In the caption:** the same tag in the first line, before the "more" cut.
- **Platform tool:** enable the paid partnership label as well. It does not replace the above.

This applies even if access was gifted rather than paid.
