# Frame of Reference Options

The frame is the single strategic decision to lock before any copy is written. It decides which
comparison a visitor runs in their head, and therefore which axis we get judged on.

## The three strategies

| Strategy | When it works | Cost | Fit for Become today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Head-to-head in an existing category | You beat the incumbent on the category's core axis | You inherit the incumbent's table stakes | Poor. We do not beat Hevy on logging depth or MyFitnessPal on food database size. |
| Dominate a subsegment | A well-defined slice where our strengths are the defining feature | You give up the rest of the category | **Best fit.** Cheap, honest, and defensible at our size. |
| Create a new category | You have budget and years to teach the market a new word | Enormous education spend | Poor. Pre-revenue, no paid budget. |

## Scoring the candidates

Score 1 to 5 on each axis. Higher is better. `Instant` = a stranger understands it in two seconds.
`Strengths obvious` = our attributes are the frame's defining features. `Loses on core axis` is
scored inverted (5 = we do not lose). `Headroom` = survives the next two features.

| Candidate frame | Instant | Strengths obvious | Does not lose on core axis | Headroom | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workout tracker | 5 | 1 | 1 | 2 | **9** |
| Fitness app | 5 | 1 | 2 | 3 | **11** |
| Wellness platform | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 | **12** |
| AI personal trainer | 4 | 3 | 2 | 4 | **13** |
| All-in-one fitness app | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | **15** |
| **Coach-led all-in-one training system** | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **17** |
| **Your coach, in your pocket** | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | **16** |

Scores are judgement, not measurement. The table's value is that it forces the argument to be
explicit and repeatable, not that 17 beats 16 by a real margin. Re-score it when the product
changes.

## Notes per candidate

**Workout tracker.** Do not use. The category's core axis is logging depth, history, and export.
Hevy and Strong have years of head start there. Filing here invites the one comparison we lose,
and it buries nutrition, mind, and the coach entirely.

**Fitness app.** True and useless. Every alternative on the list is also a fitness app, so the
frame does zero work.

**Wellness platform.** Vague, corporate, and invites comparison with everything from Headspace to
a gym chain. Also drifts toward the abstract register the voice rules ban.

**AI personal trainer.** Tempting because the generator is real. Two problems. First, it invites
comparison with a general AI chatbot, which is free and instant, and it puts the argument on a
field where "it's just a wrapper" is the obvious objection. Second, it buries the coach, which is
the credibility asset we actually have. Use the AI generator as an attribute, not as the frame.

**All-in-one fitness app.** Honest and close. The weakness is that "all-in-one" is a crowded claim
and reads as a compromise: worse than each specialist at its own job. It needs the coach to
survive.

**Coach-led all-in-one training system.** Strongest total. "Coach-led" is the differentiator no
app-stack alternative can copy and no chatbot can fake, and it reframes "all-in-one" from
compromise to design. Table stakes it commits us to: the programs must genuinely be Jon's, the
progression must be real, and all five hubs must be usable. We meet all three.

**Your coach, in your pocket.** The consumer-facing phrasing of the same frame. Slightly more
instant, slightly less headroom (it under-promises the nutrition and mind hubs). Useful as the
sentence a user says back to a friend, with the longer frame as the internal definition.

## Recommended shape

- **Internal frame (canvas, brief, positioning doc):** coach-led all-in-one training system.
- **External phrasing (headline, listing, share text):** the "your coach, in your pocket" register.
- **Primary alternative we position against:** the stitched app stack. It is the one most of our
  best-fit users are living with, and it is the one where all three value themes land at once.
- **Secondary alternative:** the in-person trainer, for the price and schedule argument. Handle it
  on structure, never on a price we do not have.

## What this frame costs us

- We give up the depth-logger audience. Someone who wants bar-speed data and CSV export is not
  our user, and chasing them dilutes the frame.
- We take on an obligation: the coach must be visible. If Jon disappears from the surface, the
  frame stops being true and we fall back to "all-in-one fitness app," where we are weaker.
- We commit to all five hubs working. "All-in-one" with one broken hub is worse than three good
  specialists.

## Before locking

1. Say the frame to someone outside the project and ask what they think Become does.
2. Check the frame against the next two roadmap items. If either breaks it, wait.
3. Write the locked line into `marketing/.agents/become-context.md` section 7, with a date, and
   bump the doc version. A frame change is a major bump because it invalidates existing copy.
