# Examples: Good and Bad

Paired copy for every Become surface. The ❌ versions are the ones a generic marketing agent writes.
The ✅ versions are what ships. Read the "why" column: the fix is almost always the same three moves.
Name the mechanic, cut the abstraction, shorten.

---

## Hero

❌
> **Transform your fitness journey with the all-in-one platform**
> Become seamlessly unites training, nutrition, and mindset so you can effortlessly unlock your
> potential and finally achieve the results you deserve.
> `[ Start your transformation ]`

Why it fails: banned words in three of four lines, four ideas in the lead, zero mechanics, a CTA that
sounds heavier than typing an email address, and a promise ("results you deserve") we cannot make.

✅
> **The only fitness app your goal actually needs.**
> Coach-built programs, photo nutrition logging, live set tracking, and daily mind work, organized
> into one plan so you know what to do next.
> `[ Start today ]`
> No credit card. We email you a sign-in link.

Why it works: one idea, mechanics named in the reader's words, the CTA states time not commitment,
and the footnote pre-answers the two objections that stall a signup.

## Feature block: LIVE mode

❌
> **Next-generation workout tracking**
> Our revolutionary training engine leverages intelligent data capture to deliver a real-time,
> AI-powered logging experience for the modern athlete.

✅
> **Log the set before you rack the bar.**
> LIVE mode holds one set on the screen. The fields are blank, last session's 185 × 8 sits right
> under them, and tapping done starts the rest timer for you.

Why: the ✅ names what is physically on the screen. Blank fields with last session's numbers
underneath is a real design decision (Become never pre-fills a set you did not do) and it is the kind
of detail nobody invents in a generic draft. Details are what make copy feel like it came from
someone who trains.

## Feature block: photo nutrition

❌
> **Effortless meal tracking**
> Simply snap a photo and let our advanced AI do the rest. It is that easy.

✅
> **One photo. The whole plate, itemized.**
> Chicken, rice, and the sauce come back as separate lines with calories and macros. Scan a barcode
> when it came out of a box.

Why: "let our AI do the rest" is a promise with no shape. Listing what comes back tells the reader
exactly what they get and quietly proves the mechanic is real.

## Feature block: the weekly recap

❌
> **Track your progress like never before**
> Visualize your data with beautiful charts and gain powerful insights into your fitness journey.

✅
> **Your week, written back to you.**
> Sessions logged, volume moved, streak held, how the days felt. Sunday night, in one screen.

Why: "insights" is the emptiest noun in software marketing. The ✅ lists the four things that are
actually in the recap.

## Buttons and microcopy

| ❌ | ✅ | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Submit | Send my link | Owns the mechanic instead of describing our plumbing |
| Get started free | Start today | "Free" reads like a trial that ends; nothing is gated |
| Learn more | See what's inside | Names the payoff of the scroll |
| Sign up now! | Sign up with your email | States the entire cost of the action |
| Continue | Next: your equipment | Tells them what they are walking into |
| Try Become risk-free | No credit card. No password. | Two facts beat one adjective |

## Ad copy

❌
> Sick of apps that don't work? Become is the #1 all-in-one fitness app trusted by thousands. Join
> now and transform your body in 30 days. Limited spots available!

Four separate violations: a fabricated rank, a fabricated user count, a promised timeline and result,
and fake scarcity.

✅
> Your program is in one app. Your food is in another. Your notes are in a third.
> Become runs all of it: coach-built phases, a photo of your plate that itemizes itself, and set
> logging that already knows what you lifted last time.
> Sign up with your email. Nothing to pay.

## Share-sheet line

❌ "Check out this awesome fitness app I'm using! 💪"

✅ "This is my week in Become: four sessions, protein hit five days, streak at 12."

Why: the ✅ is legible in a group chat with no image, it is about the sender rather than the app, and
it is honest because the numbers are the sender's own. Never render another user's data into a share
artifact.

---

## The three moves, restated

1. **Name the mechanic.** If the sentence would still be true of a rival app, it is not our sentence.
2. **Cut the abstraction.** Swap every abstract noun (journey, experience, insights, potential,
   solution, platform) for the thing on screen.
3. **Shorten.** Delete adverbs, then adjectives, then the second idea. Copy that fits gets read.

## The truth pass, restated

Before returning any of the above, check each line against these five questions. One "no" is a
rewrite, not a note.

1. Is this literally true on `become.redbtn.io` today?
2. Does it avoid pricing, tiers, trials, and discounts entirely?
3. Does it avoid any user count, rating, testimonial, or result claim?
4. Does it avoid a promised timeline, a pound count, a medical claim, and any before/after framing?
5. Would the reader still endorse the technique if we explained it to them?
