# Voice Registers

Two registers, never blended inside one block. This file is the paired-example bank.

---

## The split

| | Product voice | Coach voice |
|---|---|---|
| Person | Second person, "you" | First person, "I" |
| Leads with | Mechanism | Experience |
| Sentence shape | Short, declarative, front-loaded verb | Conversational, varied length |
| Proof | The product does it | I have watched this happen |
| Opinions | None | Encouraged, on training |
| Owns | Landing page, in-app, brand handle, listings, ads | Jon's handle, talking heads, replies, coach posts |
| CTA style | Verb plus object plus payoff | A question, or send this to someone |

**Where "we" belongs:** almost nowhere. "We" collapses the split. Use "I" for Jon and name the
product plainly when the product is the subject.

## Blends that break the split

❌ "Jon here. Become empowers you to unlock consistent training."
✅ "I built the thing I kept asking clients to do by hand."

❌ "We're excited to announce our new LIVE mode!"
✅ Brand: "LIVE mode keeps last session's numbers under the set you are about to do." / Jon: "I have written a lot of weights on a lot of index cards. This is better than the cards."

❌ Jon: "Log your set and see your PR history."
✅ Jon: "You will not remember what you did last Tuesday. It will."

❌ Product: "I think three days a week is enough for most people."
✅ Product: "Three training days a week is a valid program." / Jon: "Three days a week progresses slower than four. It also actually happens."

## Structural rules

**Sentence length.** Vary it. Two short, one long, one very short. Uniform sentence length is the
single loudest tell that copy was generated rather than said.

**Front-load the noun.** "The plan holds where you left off" beats "Where you left off is held by
the plan."

**Contractions.** Jon uses them naturally in speech. In written captions keep them light; the
voice reads as considered rather than casual.

**Openers to avoid entirely:** "Let's talk about," "Here's the thing," "Real talk," "Listen,"
"I'm going to be honest with you." All of them delay the first idea by a full second, which is
the whole hook window.

**Closers that work:** a question he actually wants answered, a send instruction that names the
recipient, or nothing at all. A post that ends on the idea is stronger than a post that ends on
a CTA it did not earn.

**Emoji.** At most one, only if it carries meaning. Never as punctuation, never as a bullet.

**Em dashes.** Near zero in deliverable copy. Use a period, a comma, or a colon.

---

## Banned in both registers

"journey," "unlock your potential," "game-changer," "revolutionary," "seamless," "effortless,"
"10x," "crush it," "no excuses," "beast mode," "grind," "just," "simply," any hustle or shame
framing, any promised outcome, any invented price.
