# Reply Patterns

Structure for every reply: **acknowledge what they actually said, answer concretely, one next
step.** Under four sentences. Written as Jon, first person.

Replies are public coaching. Everyone reading the thread is deciding whether this coach is worth
following, and the person who asked is only one of them.

---

## By comment type

### Skeptic

They are testing whether you will get defensive. Agree with the true part first.

❌ "You should actually try it before judging."
✅ "Fair. Most of them are the same. The one difference here is the food and the training sit in the same week, so there is nothing to reconcile on Sunday."

### Beginner

Give a specific starting point, not encouragement.

❌ "Just start your journey and the rest will follow."
✅ "Three days a week, the beginner program, and log every set even when it feels pointless. The logging is what makes week six make sense."

### Returning after a break

Never reference the gap as a failure.

❌ "Time to get back on it. No excuses this time."
✅ "Start at the beginning of the phase, not where you stopped. It will feel too easy for about a week, and that is the point."

### Injury or pain

The referral response. Every time, no exceptions. See `references/claims-boundaries.md`.

❌ "Sounds like it might just be tight hips, try stretching before you lift."
✅ "I am not going to guess at that over a comment. Get someone who can actually look at it to look at it. Once you know what you are dealing with, send it to me and we will build the training around it."

### Price

Short. Do not oversell a free thing.

❌ "It's 100% free right now, get in while you can before we start charging!"
✅ "Free today. Email link, no card."

### Competitor comparison

Be generous about them, specific about us.

❌ "We do everything they do but better."
✅ "They are good at what they do. The difference here is that the plate you logged and the set you did are in the same week view."

### Feature request

Say yes, no, or not today. Never "great idea, stay tuned."

❌ "Love this idea! Stay tuned."
✅ "Not available today. I want it too. If you tell me how you would use it, that helps decide where it lands."

### Hostile or bad faith

One reply maximum, then stop. Never argue in public twice.

❌ A three-paragraph defense.
✅ "Not for everyone, and that is fine." Or nothing at all.

### Compliment

Accept it and hand back something useful.

❌ "Thank you so much! 🙏🙏"
✅ "Appreciate it. If it is working, the thing worth protecting is the logging habit, not the streak."

### Question you cannot answer

Say so.

❌ A confident guess.
✅ "I do not know. I will find out and come back to this."

## DM openers

When someone comments a keyword (`BECOME`, `LIVE`, `PLATE`, `WEEK1`), the DM must open with the
promised thing before anything else. A DM that opens with a pitch is why keyword flows die.

**`LIVE`**
> "Here is the LIVE setup: open the session, tap the exercise, and the set is already waiting with what you lifted last time underneath it. Type the two numbers, tick it, rest timer runs itself. Link when you want it: [link]"

**`PLATE`**
> "Photo logging: one picture of the whole plate, not each item. It itemizes what is there and you correct anything it missed, then it lands against your targets. Link: [link]"

**`WEEK1`**
> "Week one is three sessions. The big lifts carry a demo clip you can watch mid-set, and you log the sets as you go. Nothing to set up beyond your training days. Link: [link]"

**`BECOME`**
> "Training, food, mood and progress in one app. Free today, email link, no card. [link]"

Rules: the promised thing first, the link second, no third message unless they reply.

## Story answers

Question-sticker answers are the cheapest coaching content that exists and they feed the Coach
Answer slot.

- Screenshot the question, answer in text over it, keep it to two sentences.
- Answer the question that was asked, not the one you wish had been asked.
- Anything medical goes to the referral response, even in a story.
- Never screenshot a question that identifies a person by anything more than a first name.
- The best three answers each week become the next Coach Answer Reel. Keep a running list.

## What never appears in a reply

- A client's name, situation, or result.
- A number about anyone's body.
- A price, a discount, or a hint that one is coming.
- A medical opinion.
- A promise about a timeline.
- Sarcasm at the expense of the person asking.
- More than one next step.
