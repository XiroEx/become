# Competitor Dossiers

Starting notes per competitor. **Nothing here is verified.** Every entry is a research starting
point, not a fact to quote. Verify with `WebSearch` and `WebFetch` and stamp the date before any
claim leaves this file.

**No pricing appears in this file on purpose.** Pricing changes constantly, a stale number in our
copy is a liability, and Become has no price to compare against anyway. Check pricing live, use it
only for internal understanding of their model, and never publish it in our copy.

Dossier status legend: `unverified` (never checked) · `checked YYYY-MM-DD` · `stale` (older than
90 days).

---

## Hevy — status: unverified

**What it is.** A workout logger with a strong social layer.
**Why our users consider it.** Best-in-class set logging, history, and progression charts.
**Their likely axis of strength.** Logging depth and history. **Structural.** Years of product
focus and user data. Do not attack.
**Their likely positional cost.** It logs what you decided to do. It does not decide.
**Our opening hypothesis.** The plan, not the log. Also nutrition and mind, which they do not touch.
**Verify:** category word in their own hero, whether they have added any programming or AI
generation, social-feed prominence.

## Strong — status: unverified

**What it is.** A long-running workout logger.
**Why our users consider it.** Simplicity and habit. Many people have years of data in it.
**Strength.** Installed base and data lock-in. **Structural.**
**Positional cost.** Same as Hevy: it is a record, not a plan.
**Our opening hypothesis.** Do not ask for a rip-and-replace. Ask for one week with the plan
attached.

## MyFitnessPal — status: unverified

**What it is.** The default calorie and macro tracker.
**Why our users consider it.** The food database is the biggest there is.
**Strength.** Database size, built from years of user contribution. **Structural.** We will not
out-database it and should never claim to.
**Positional cost.** Logging is search-and-tap heavy, which is the single most common reason people
stop logging food.
**Our opening hypothesis.** The photo itemizes the plate. Concede the database, change the axis to
effort per meal.
**Verify:** current photo or AI logging capability, since this is the axis we would compete on.

## Cal AI — status: unverified

**What it is.** Photo-first calorie logging.
**Why our users consider it.** Same job as our nutrition hub, done by the same mechanic.
**Strength.** Focus. Their whole product is the thing our one hub does.
**Positional cost.** It is only nutrition. No training, no plan, no coach.
**Our opening hypothesis.** The plate lands next to the training that earned it.
**Verify carefully:** whether their photo logging itemizes multiple items on one plate, since that
is the specific capability we claim. If it does, our claim narrows to the integration, not the
mechanic. This is the single most important verification in the whole dossier set.

## Fitbod — status: unverified

**What it is.** Algorithmic workout generation.
**Why our users consider it.** It decides the session for you, which is our generator's job.
**Strength.** Generation quality and recovery modelling. Possibly **structural** if it rests on a
large training dataset.
**Positional cost.** No human coach behind it, and no nutrition or mind surface.
**Our opening hypothesis.** Coach-built programs *and* a generator, rather than an algorithm alone.
The credibility asset is Jon.

## Ladder — status: partially observed via `marketing/inspo-analysis.md`

**What it is.** Coach-led team training, sold on named coaches and structured programs.
**Why it matters most.** It is the closest analogue to our positioning: coach-led, program-first.
Study it hardest.
**Observed creative.** A five-slide carousel on meal-logging modalities, one method per slide with
an identical lower-third lockup, and quantified self-deprecation on accuracy that reads premium.
See `inspo-library`.
**Strength.** Named coaches with existing audiences, and team or cohort structure. Partly
**structural**, since the coach roster took time and money to assemble.
**Positional cost.** Team structure implies a schedule and a social commitment some people
specifically do not want.
**Our opening hypothesis.** One coach's system, no team to keep up with, and the nutrition and mind
surfaces attached.

## STNDRD — status: creative observed via `marketing/inspo-analysis.md`

**What it is.** A creator-led fitness app built around Chris Bumstead.
**Why it matters.** It is the clearest example of the creator-led model we are a smaller version
of, and its Instagram Story ad system is the most complete creative reference we have. 25 story
ads are analysed in `marketing/inspo-analysis.md`.
**Observed creative system.** Feature-list stories (one hub per frame, headline plus four to six
arrow bullets, phone bleeding off the right edge), feature heroes in huge type, and "App Tip"
how-to sequences with a branded pill badge, one tap per frame, and hand-drawn circles on the exact
control. The App Tip sequences are the strongest asset class in the set.
**Strength.** A celebrity audience. **Structural.** Do not attempt to match reach.
**Positional cost.** Gamification and leaderboard pushes suit a competitive audience, not a
restarter who feels behind.
**Our opening hypothesis.** Coach-led without the shame mechanics. No leaderboard that punishes.

## Whoop — status: unverified

**What it is.** A wearable and recovery-metrics subscription.
**Why our users consider it.** Data-driven recovery and sleep.
**Strength.** Hardware and physiological data. **Structural** and out of scope.
**Positional cost.** It measures. It does not program your training or your food.
**Our opening hypothesis.** Not a competitor for the same job. Do not position against it. If it
comes up, treat it as complementary and move on.

## Strava — status: unverified

**What it is.** Endurance activity tracking with a strong social graph.
**Why our users consider it.** Running and cycling, plus the social layer.
**Strength.** Network effects. **Structural.**
**Positional cost.** Endurance-first. Strength programming and nutrition are not its job.
**Our opening hypothesis.** Different sport, different job. Rarely worth a comparison.

## Headspace and Calm — status: unverified

**What they are.** Guided meditation and sleep.
**Why our users consider them.** They occupy the mind slot in the stitched stack.
**Strength.** Content library depth and brand. **Structural.**
**Positional cost.** They know nothing about your training week, so the mind practice floats free
of everything else.
**Our opening hypothesis.** Mind practice attached to the week that produced the mood. Concede
library depth openly; we are not a meditation app.

## Local coach-led alternatives (in-person trainers, small gym programming)

**What it is.** A human, in a room, with a standing appointment.
**Strength.** Accountability and real-time adaptation. **Structural**, and genuinely better at what
it is.
**Positional cost.** Cost, schedule, geography, and it ends when the sessions end.
**Our opening hypothesis.** Structure without the standing appointment. Never claim to replace a
human coach, and never make the argument about money.
